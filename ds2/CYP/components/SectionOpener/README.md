# SectionOpener

Half a page of photo beside half a page of colour, with an icon and a name in capitals. It opens the part of a chapter about one place or one section. The consumer provides one portrait photo and the name.

- A place takes its own colour class from `ColourKey` and the `map-pin` icon.
- A section takes `cyp-c-blue` and its own icon from `ColourKey`.
- Icon and title are white, or `ink` on Minsundu and Mary Christine. The class sets it.
- Title in `opener-title`: two lines at most. No other text on the colour.
