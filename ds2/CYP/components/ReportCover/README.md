# ReportCover

First page of the narrative report, A4 portrait: the blue ground with the seal on its white plate, the title in capitals, the subtitle in `yellow`, the date disc, one photo to the bottom edge, the chitenge strip. The consumer provides the title (three lines at most), the subtitle (one line), the month and year, and one landscape photo.

- Title in `cover-title`, white. Subtitle in Ubuntu Bold 26px, `yellow`, under a white hairline.
- The date disc is `yellow` with `ink` text and overlaps the edge between the blue ground and the photo.
- Replace the hatched slot with `<img>` inside `.cyp-photo`. One photo, no collage, no text over it.
- Partner or donor logos go on the same white plate, under the seal.
