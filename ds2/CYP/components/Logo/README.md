# Logo

The project seal and the three app wordmarks, as files in the Logos asset group. The consumer provides nothing: place the file with `<img>` and give it an `alt`.

- The seal (`cyp-seal.png`) signs reports, covers and internal templates. Smallest width 48px on screen, 15mm in print.
- A wordmark (`cyp-edu.svg`, `cyp-fin.svg`, `cyp-eat.svg`) names its app in the app bar and on the documents that app makes. Smallest height 24px.
- The app icons are for the phone home screen only.
- On `blue`, `navy`, a category colour or a photo, put the logo on a white plate (`.cyp-plate`). The seal file has a white ground and no transparent version.
- Do not redraw, recolour, stretch or crop a logo. Do not set the letters CYP in a font to stand in for a wordmark.
