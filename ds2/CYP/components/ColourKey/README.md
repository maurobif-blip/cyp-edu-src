- Tool buttons carry their icon before the label, in the colour of the label, with no disc.
- Colour means place. Places always appear in the order of the table, also when a chart shows only some of them.
- Names are written as in the tables: Luigi and Minsundu without the word Centre, Nkwazi Centre with it.
- A highlight colour means outcome. It is used in the narrative report only.
- A section is told by its icon and its name. Sections share `blue` and have no colour of their own. A chart that compares sections uses `blue` bars with the names beside them.
- A unit listed under Also covers takes the icon of its section and keeps its own name: Football shows the Sports icon and the word Football.
- UCC Children Section shows the `puzzle` icon on a `blue` disc wherever sections are listed: budget lines, requests, filters, forms, tables by section. The Minsundu colour appears only where the row, bar or slice stands for the place Minsundu. If a chart by place splits Minsundu into UCC and Youth, both bars are Minsundu yellow with the names beside them. The puzzle never sits on a yellow disc.
- The Literacy class is held at Minsundu and follows the same rule.
- A place shows its icon on a disc in its own colour, wherever it needs a disc or an opener icon. A place without its own icon uses `map-pin`. Place icons never show what the children went through: no street, no hardship.
- A place icon is never used for a section, and no section icon is used for a place. Nkwazi Centre takes `bird` because `sprout` already means Social enterprises and outcome 2.
- A new place or section is added here first, then used. Do not pick a colour or an icon inside an app or a report.
- Put this block once in the narrative report, after the contents page.
