# ChitengeStrip

The zig-zag band from the app wordmarks, as a full-width strip: `<div class="cyp-chitenge"></div>` (24px) or with `cyp-chitenge--tall` (72px). The consumer provides only the container width.

- One strip per page or screen, flush to the bottom edge, never as a divider between blocks.
- Use it at the foot of covers, chapter openers, internal templates and the sign-in screen of an app.
- Both heights show one full repeat of the pattern: the tile is 16 by 24px in the thin strip and 48 by 72px in the tall one. Do not stretch it, rotate it or change its colours.
- Outside this strip and the wordmarks, the four chitenge colours are not used.
