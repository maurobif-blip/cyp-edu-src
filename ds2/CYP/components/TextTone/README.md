# TextTone

A figure that takes the colour of its meaning. The consumer provides the figure, the word that names it, and the tone.

- `cyp-text-ok` for what is left or within the plan: Left.
- `cyp-text-warn` for what is late or close to the limit: Late.
- `cyp-text-danger` for what is short or over: Short by, Over by.
- The word is always beside or above the figure. The colour never stands alone.
- Use the tone on the figure only, at `heading-3` size or larger. Labels, notes and body text stay `ink` or `ink-muted`.
- A figure with no judgement stays `blue` in a `StatTile` and `ink` elsewhere.
